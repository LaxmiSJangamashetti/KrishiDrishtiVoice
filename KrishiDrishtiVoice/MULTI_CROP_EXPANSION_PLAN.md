# Multi-Crop Expansion Plan

## Current decision

Do not advertise the 20 proposed crops as operational. Only tomato has a curated, tested text-guidance path. Every additional crop requires an evidence-backed knowledge slice, Kannada terminology review, LU aliases, retrieval tests, safety review, and farmer-facing wording review.

## Recommended order

1. Rice, chilli, and potato: high usefulness for Karnataka users, but only after institution-level sources and Kannada terminology are collected.
2. Maize, ragi, onion, brinjal, and okra: next expansion group after source and symptom coverage review.
3. Groundnut, cotton, sugarcane, pigeon pea, green gram, chickpea, and sunflower: crop-specific evidence and regional context required.
4. Wheat, jowar, banana, and mango: add when target-region use cases and source quality are established.

For each crop, implement observation-first records before any disease or treatment content. No pesticide dose, chemical mix, or confirmed diagnosis should be added without current local expert review.

## Required gate per crop

- Kannada and English crop aliases reviewed by a Kannada speaker.
- At least one authoritative cultivation source and source-specific claims.
- Separate symptom, pest, disease-reference, and environmental-stress records.
- Ambiguity and crop-omission tests.
- Safety and unsupported-treatment tests.
- Retrieval precision review against existing tomato behavior.
- Human agricultural review before changing readiness from planned.

Image support is a later, independent gate; text support must not imply visual support.
