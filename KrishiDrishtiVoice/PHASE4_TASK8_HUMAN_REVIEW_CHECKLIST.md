# Phase 4 Task 8 — Human Voice Review Checklist

Status: `PENDING_HUMAN_REVIEW`

For each test, record device, OS, browser, microphone permission, network, language, recording length, exact farmer utterance, exact transcript, interpretation, follow-up, response text, TTS playback quality, latency, failure reason, and reviewer notes.

Test separately:

- Kannada, English, and supported code-mixed speech.
- Regional Kannada accents and crop/pest terminology.
- Clear, fast, slow, low-volume, paused, noisy, silent, incomplete, and repeated speech.
- Corrections, negations, follow-up answers, text-to-voice switching, repeated recording submission, and New Conversation reset.

Do not manually correct transcripts in the test record. Mark live tests separately from mocked or simulated tests.
