# Phase 1 Corpus Human Review Checklist

For each pending record, reviewers must mark **approve**, **revise**, or **cannot assess**; no approval is implied by this checklist.

- [ ] Original Kannada/English/code-mixed expression is natural for the stated style and not misleadingly artificial.
- [ ] Crop, plant part, and every symptom are grounded in an exact evidence phrase.
- [ ] Symptom polarity correctly preserves negation scope.
- [ ] Multiple observations are complete and their relationships are explicit.
- [ ] Context, correction, contradiction, and follow-up state are correctly represented.
- [ ] Pest/disease labels are farmer mentions or observations, never verified diagnosis.
- [ ] Missing information and abstention are appropriate; no fact is invented.
- [ ] Safety flags identify unsafe requests without embedding advice.
- [ ] Agricultural terminology and implications are reviewed by an agricultural expert where relevant.
- [ ] Record remains `draft` until independent review is recorded.
